CausePilot turns raw field notes from relief and civic teams into donor-ready action plans in 90 seconds, so funding momentum never lags the crisis. The app ingests messy mission context, urgency windows, and constraints, then orchestrates a sequenced runway complete with KPIs, owners, experiments, and telemetry signals donors can trust. Under the hood, an AI strategy pipeline backstops real-time insight with a deterministic fallback engine, so demos stay rock-solid even without API keys. The result is a shareable, production-ready playbook that helps TreeHacks teams prove impact, secure dollars faster, and stand out to judges looking for both creativity and feasibility.
