ReliefCanvas turns chaotic field notes from disaster responders into phased playbooks, actionable supply stacks, and best-practice audits in under a minute. It layers a structured GPT-4.1-mini mission composer with a local Sphere/WHO ruleset so every output is graded against water, medical, and logistics standards automatically. A shared mission feed plus neon, kiosk-friendly UI makes it demo-ready for judges and lets teams broadcast synths straight from any tablet. With zero backend dependencies beyond Vercel + OpenAI, it’s the fastest way for response teams to prove operational readiness on stage.
